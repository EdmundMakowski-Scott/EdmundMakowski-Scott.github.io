CREATE Institute

Student Fellow at the Milwaukee School of Engineering.

What the CREATE Institute does

The CREATE Institute is an academic organization at MSOE where students are hired to teach undergraduate engineering students how to use common engineering tools, including MATLAB, 3D printing, 3D modeling, soldering, and more.

These student fellows are also in charge of constructing, maintaining, and repairing the 3D printers and machining equipment around campus.

What it has given me

This opportunity has allowed me to become closer to my student body while giving back to the community that taught me these same skills earlier in my education.

It has also let me work with 3D printers in depth, which has helped me better understand plastics material science and the science behind additive manufacturing.
